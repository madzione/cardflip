## Code in this repository from other projects

### fsrs-rs

The FSRS optimizer and simulator in `golang/scheduler/` (`optimizer_model.go`,
`optimizer_train.go`, `simulate.go`) are ported from
[fsrs-rs](https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/fsrs-rs): the model, its
parameter bounds, the training procedure and the review simulation.

Copyright (c) 2023, Open Spaced Repetition. Licensed under the BSD 3-Clause
License (see [LICENSES/BSD-3-Clause-fsrs-rs.txt](LICENSES/BSD-3-Clause-fsrs-rs.txt)).

### Anki

The Image Occlusion note type's card template and the shape colour
variables of its styling in `golang/core/notetypes.go` are reproduced
unchanged from [Anki](https://github.com/ankitects/anki)
(`rslib/src/image_occlusion/notetype.rs` and `notetype.css`), so that image
occlusion notes made in Cardflip work in Anki.

Copyright: Ankitects Pty Ltd and contributors. Licensed under the GNU Affero
General Public License, version 3 or later (see
[LICENSES/AGPL-3.0.txt](LICENSES/AGPL-3.0.txt)). Section 13 of the GNU GPL
version 3 permits combining it with Cardflip.

Everything else Cardflip does to read, write and display Anki packages and
notes is its own implementation of Anki's file formats and behaviour; Anki's
source is not part of it. `tool/check_apkg_export.py`, a development check
that is not part of the app, uses the `anki` Python package.

### fluffy

`golang/fluffy/` and `lib/golang/src/` are the message layer written by
[fluffy](https://github.com/madzione/fluffy), and `lib/golang/api.g.dart` is
generated by it.

Copyright (c) 2026 madzione. Licensed under the MIT License (see
[LICENSES/MIT-fluffy.txt](LICENSES/MIT-fluffy.txt)).

## Dependencies

Dependencies are not part of this repository; the build fetches them and
links them into the app. Each one's license applies to it, and a
distributed build must carry their license texts.

- **Dart and Flutter packages** (`pubspec.lock`): MIT, BSD-3-Clause and
  Apache-2.0, except `dbus` and `nm` (MPL-2.0, used unmodified; source at
  [pub.dev](https://pub.dev)). Flutter collects their licenses, and the
  Flutter engine's, for the app's license page.
- **Go modules** (`golang/go.mod`): MIT, BSD-3-Clause and Apache-2.0. The
  ones the app links are compiled into its Go library and, for the web,
  `golang.wasm`, together with the Go standard library and `wasm_exec.js`

  them with their license texts. `modernc.org/sqlite` and its dependencies,
  among them `github.com/hashicorp/golang-lru/v2` (MPL-2.0), are used only
  by tests and are not part of the app.
- **libmpv and FFmpeg**, for audio and video. `media_kit_libs_video`
  bundles builds of them with the app on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android;
  on Linux Cardflip uses the system's libmpv. The bundled builds are LGPL
  builds: libmpv is built with `-Dgpl=false` and is licensed under the GNU
  LGPL version 2.1 or later (see
  [LICENSES/LGPL-2.1.txt](LICENSES/LGPL-2.1.txt)), and FFmpeg is built with
  `--disable-gpl --disable-nonfree --enable-version3` and is licensed under
  the GNU LGPL version 3 or later (see
  [LICENSES/LGPL-3.0.txt](LICENSES/LGPL-3.0.txt), which builds on the GPL
  version 3 in [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This is what the libraries report about
  themselves in the builds media_kit downloads (Windows `2023-09-24`,
  Android `v1.1.7`, macOS and iOS `v0.6.0`). Their source and build scripts:
  [Windows](https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-win32-video-build),
  [Android](https://github.com/media-kit/libmpv-android-video-build),
